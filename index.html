Designing playful robots to live with

Hi, I'm Borui. I explore the relationships people can have with robots when the robots aren't just serving us: when they're idle, curious, or busy with a task of their own. I research, sketch and build these experiences end to end, from fieldwork to working hardware.

Selected Work

Windborne

An installation that turns a wave of the hand into drifting light and soap bubbles, bringing a breath of wind into hospital waiting areas.

  • RoleDesigner & builder · 2-person team
  • MethodsSpeculative design, rapid prototyping, survey (n=30), in-person sessions
  • OutcomeDemo at ICMI '26 · 21 of 30 participants reported a mood lift
Read the case study →

FlowCart

A modular robot cart for hotel housekeepers that greets, yields and says goodbye using hotel etiquette instead of screens.

  • RolePrototyping lead · 3-person team
  • MethodsShadowing & interviews at a 150-room hotel, Wizard-of-Oz testing
  • OutcomeWorking robot · HRI '26 Companion paper
Read the case study →

iContour

A web platform where radiation-oncology residents practice outlining tumors and get instant feedback against an expert's contour.

  • RoleUI/UX designer & full-stack developer · UC San Diego
  • MethodsWeekly sessions with 5 clinicians for 2 semesters, iterative redesign
  • OutcomePilot with 9 residents at 5 institutions · CHI EA, DIS, IJROBP
Read the case study →

UW Medicine

Redesigning how patients find care across 4 hospitals and 300+ clinics, for people of any medical or technical background.

  • RoleUX designer · 4-person capstone team · real client
  • MethodsField study, 6 think-aloud tests, affinity mapping, 2 prototype rounds
  • OutcomePlain-language search & location page template in Figma
