OKAPI Reusables

Making a reusable-cup service work for people with and without a phone.

At a Glance

OKAPI lends reusable cups through partner cafés. But to borrow one you had to scan a QR code with your own phone, and then remember to bring the cup back. With OKAPI as our client, our team redesigned borrowing and returning so it's easier and works for more people.

Context

Summer project with a real client, OKAPI Reusables

Timeline

Summer 2023

Team

3 people

My contribution

User research, the lo-fi prototype, and the design of the physical membership card

Methods

Competitive analysis (6 services), user research, user flows, information architecture, lo-fi and hi-fi prototyping, weekly client reviews

What We Learned

  • Convenience decides everything. People forget to return cups and won't go out of their way to do it; more return points matter.
  • Cost adds up. Bubble tea already costs about $6, so any extra fee feels heavy. Discounts work better than deposits.
  • Not everyone has a phone at hand, and requiring one excludes people.

The Decision: Two Paths, One Service

A redesigned app and a membership card

WhyAn app-only flow would keep leaving out people without a phone. So we went with two paths. One is an app redesign: scan to borrow or return in about 3 seconds, see which cups you have out, and track your impact and rewards. The other is a $10 tap-to-borrow membership card for people without a phone. In our journey map, frustration dropped and stayed above neutral the whole way through.

The full process, from research and storyboards to the hi-fi prototype, is in the project PDF.
