RetroConnect

Dial from the past into the future: a vintage telephone that browses the web.

The Idea

Wireless data flows all around us, invisible. RetroConnect makes it tangible by putting it somewhere it never belonged: an old desk telephone. Type a URL on the original keypad, and the phone fetches the site over Wi-Fi, scrolls its text across a small LCD and beeps in step with how slow the site is to respond.

Context

Solo project for DXARTS 470: Sensing and Control Systems for Digital Arts, UW

Timeline

Sep – Dec 2023 (11 weeks)

Built with

Arduino UNO, ESP8266 Wi-Fi module, SparkFun SerLCD 1602, a RadioShack DUOFONE 210

Role

Everything: concept, reverse-engineering the keypad, wiring, firmware

The Build

The hardest part was making the phone's own keypad talk to an Arduino. I mapped every key on the phone's two 10-pin connectors by hand, then kept the original handset speaker for the beeps. Wiring diagrams, the key map and build notes are in the GitHub repository.

What I Learned

This was my first real look at how experimental art works. Looking back, I still approached it like an engineer, starting from a technical problem to solve rather than from what I wanted people to feel.
